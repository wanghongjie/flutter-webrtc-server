Membership Benefits & How to Cancel
RePhone Security Membership (International)
Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Edition: International (App Store / Google Play)
In one sentence
International memberships are auto-renewing subscriptions billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). Benefits start immediately after purchase. You can cancel anytime in the store where you subscribed — cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
Contents

1. When do benefits take effect?

1.1 Usually immediately

  1. You complete the purchase in the App Store / Google Play;
  2. The app sends the store receipt to our server;
  3. Our server validates the receipt with Apple / Google (we never trust the device alone);
  4. Once validated, premium features are unlocked — normally within a few seconds.

Your membership page shows the active plan and the expiry date. If it does not refresh, pull to refresh or re-open the Membership page.

1.2 Membership duration

  • Monthly: active for one month and renews automatically every month.
  • Yearly: active for one year and renews automatically every year.
  • Renewal happens automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.

1.3 Paid but premium not showing?

  1. Close and re-open the Membership page (it re-syncs with the server).
  2. Tap Restore Purchases on the Membership page — this re-sends your store receipt to our server.
  3. Confirm you are signed in with the same email account used for the purchase.
  4. Wait a few minutes and retry (store callbacks can be delayed occasionally).
  5. Still missing? Email dahongwudi123@gmail.com with your account email + store order ID.
Switching between monthly and yearly
Switching plans is handled by Apple / Google as an upgrade or downgrade inside the same subscription group. Downgrades usually take effect at the end of the current period.

3. How to cancel your subscription

3.1 On iPhone / iPad (App Store)

  1. Open iOS Settings;
  2. Tap your Apple ID name at the top;
  3. Tap Subscriptions;
  4. Select RePhone Security → Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial).

3.2 On Android (Google Play)

  1. Open the Google Play Store;
  2. Tap your profile icon (top right);
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions;
  4. Select RePhone Security → Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.

3.3 What happens after cancelling

  • You keep premium access until the end of the current billing period;
  • You will not be charged again after that date;
  • Cancelling is not the same as a refund — see section 4;
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; please cancel in the store.

4. Refunds

International purchases are billed by Apple / Google, so refunds follow their policies:

  • Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (sign in → Report a problem);
  • Google: request a refund in Play Store → Order history, or via Google Play Help;
  • You may also contact dahongwudi123@gmail.com — we can help verify usage, but the final decision belongs to the store.

If a refund is granted, the corresponding membership time is revoked.

5. Other questions

5.1 New phone or reinstalled app

Sign in with the same email, then tap Restore Purchases on the Membership page — no need to buy again.

5.2 Restore Purchases found nothing

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID / Google account that made the purchase, and with the same RePhone account email.

5.3 Does the camera phone need a membership?

No. The camera device requires no sign-in and no purchase; premium applies to the monitoring account.
