User Guide: Sign Up, Sign In & Bind a Camera
Getting Started with RePhone Security (International)
Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Edition: International (App Store / Google Play)
Two phones, two roles
Monitor: the phone you use every day. It requires an email sign-up and is used to watch the live view.
Camera: the spare phone you place where you want monitoring. It needs no sign-up and no sign-in — just scan the QR code shown by the monitor.
Contents

1. Create an account (monitor phone)

  1. Open RePhone Security, accept the Terms and Privacy Policy, then tap Sign in with email.
  2. Enter your email address (e.g. name@example.com) and tap Next.
  3. We check whether the email is registered:
    • Not registered → a 6-digit verification code is sent (valid for about 10 minutes);
    • Already registered → you go straight to the password sign-in screen.
  4. Enter the code you received.
  5. Set a password: at least 8 characters, containing both letters and numbers; confirm it and submit.
No verification code?
Check your spam / promotions folder · make sure the address is typed correctly · the code expires after 10 minutes (request a new one) · some corporate mail servers block external mail, so a personal address works better.

2. Sign in and password management

2.1 Sign in

Enter your email + password. Your session is kept, so the app opens signed in next time.

2.2 Forgot password

  1. Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen;
  2. Enter your registered email and request a code;
  3. Enter the code plus a new password (≥ 8 characters, letters + numbers);
  4. Sign in with the new password.

2.3 Change password

Profile → General settings → Reset password: enter your current password + new password + confirmation. The same page also holds language, permissions, account deletion and sign out.

3. Bind a camera (the main step)

Prepare two phones: A = monitor (signed in) and B = camera (spare phone with the app installed).

3.1 Show the QR code on the monitor (A)

  1. Sign in on phone A and open the Cameras tab;
  2. Tap the + button at the bottom right;
  3. A QR code with your monitor email appears — leave this screen open.

3.2 Scan it with the camera phone (B)

  1. Open the app on phone B and tap Bind by scanning QR code (allow the camera permission);
  2. Scan the QR code displayed on phone A;
  3. Phone B shows binding succeeded and enters camera mode, displaying "Connected to server (ID: …)";
  4. Within a few seconds the new camera appears automatically in the list on phone A — tap it to watch live.
"Invalid QR format"?
The camera phone only accepts the RePhone QR code that contains an email address (from Cameras → + ). Any other QR code will be rejected.

3.3 After binding

  • Grant permissions: Camera, Microphone and Notifications, otherwise capture will not work.
  • Keep it online: follow FAQ: Keeping the Camera Phone Online (disable battery optimization, allow background activity).
  • Rename / set location: in the camera list → Settings → change name and location (e.g. "Living room").
  • Remove a device: the same settings page can delete the binding; the camera phone then has to scan again.

4. Switching roles on a phone

Use the role menu at the top-left to switch between monitor and camera:

  • Switching to camera signs you out and asks you to scan the monitor's QR code;
  • Switching to monitor requires signing in with your email.

Recommendation: leave the spare phone permanently in camera mode.

5. Can I bind several cameras?

Yes. Repeat section 3 for each camera. The list shows each device's online status, name / location and last-seen time, with per-device playback and settings.

6. Troubleshooting

SymptomCause & fix
Camera always offline Its background was frozen by the system. Follow the keep-alive FAQ (battery optimization + background activity) and keep the app in the foreground with its notification visible.
Device visible but the picture is black or never loads Check that the lens is not covered and no other app is using the camera; check both networks; restart the camera app.
Binding failed after scanning Make sure the monitor is signed in and online; go back and generate the QR code again.
Devices disappeared on a new phone Sign in with the same email — bindings follow the account. The camera phone needs to scan once again.
Let someone else watch the same camera Bindings are per account: their camera phone can scan the QR code of the same monitor email.
